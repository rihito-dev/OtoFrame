The demo bundles **Geist** and **Geist Mono**, obtained from [vercel/geist-font](https://github.com/vercel/geist-font) at revision `10dc7658f13c38a474cde201bb09a4617267545b`.

Copyright 2024 The Geist Project Authors (https://github.com/vercel/geist-font).

These fonts are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1**, rather than the repository's MIT license. The complete font license is in [demo/fonts/OFL.txt](demo/fonts/OFL.txt) and is embedded as a comment in the generated standalone HTML. The bundled WOFF2 files are unmodified. No external font request is made at runtime.

The layout references [Taste skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/d237dd399d57524945c0c81327da5da19f7a468d/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md). Its source is not bundled, and the implementation uses original native HTML/CSS. The specific design decisions are recorded in [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md).
